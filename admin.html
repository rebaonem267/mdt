Leave "Status" blank for a normal, available date. Type anything you like to show a small badge on that date on the Tickets page — e.g. Sold Out, 20 Tickets Left, Selling Fast. Typing "sold out" (any case) also automatically stops that date from being selected on the Tickets page.

The Lemon Squeezy Variant ID links this ticket type to a specific product in your Lemon Squeezy store (Products > your product > Variants). Add as many ticket types as you like — each with its own variant — and customers will be able to select and pay for more than one type in the same visit. Use only the ID itself (letters, numbers, dashes) — not a full URL.
